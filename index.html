The problem

Smart homes aren't smart.
They're just numerous.

The modern home adds devices faster than it adds intelligence. Without a shared layer, value is lost at the seams.

01 — Fragmentation

A different app for everything

Every new device adds another app, another account, another silo that doesn't know the others exist.

02 — Exposure

More endpoints, less oversight

Each connected device is a door into the home network — and most homes have no way to watch all of them at once.

03 — Waste

Systems that can't coordinate

Heating, lighting, and power systems that don't talk to each other can't optimize together — only in isolation.

Our approach

Four principles guide everything we build.

MOD.01 — Interoperability⇄

One layer, any device

A coordination layer that speaks to devices across brands and protocols, instead of asking the home to standardize around one vendor.

MOD.02 — Privacy by default◈

Home data stays home

Processing happens as close to the home as possible. Less data leaves by default, not as an opt-in setting buried in a menu.

MOD.03 — Energy intelligence⚡

Coordination, not just reporting

Systems that adjust each other in real time to cut waste — not dashboards that just tell you how much you used.

MOD.04 — Simplicity✦

Invisible by design

The best automation disappears into daily life. No new app to check, no new habit to build.
